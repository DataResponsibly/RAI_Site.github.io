On **September 15**, [Julia Stoyanovich](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/julia-stoyanovich) joined Paul Ford, co-founder of [Aboard](https://aboard.com/), for a conversation about what it means to build and use AI responsibly, and who gets to decide what “responsible” means.

The discussion explored who bears responsibility when AI systems cause harm, the “magical thinking” surrounding AI, and the different perspectives technologists and policymakers bring to AI governance. Julia also shared what responsible AI can look like in practice for companies developing and deploying AI systems.
