*“The question isn’t whether machines can be conscious. It’s where these systems belong in our lives, and whether they’re doing what we want them to do.”* — Julia Stoyanovich

On **May 29**, Julia Stoyanovich joined host Nailea Devora on an episode of *ease*, *“What Everyone Should Know About AI,”* to break down what artificial intelligence actually is, how it works, and why understanding it matters.

From robot vacuums and chess to ELIZA, the first therapy chatbot, and her grandfather calculating Sputnik trajectories, Julia used stories and humor to take the magic out of AI and remind us that we are ultimately in control.
