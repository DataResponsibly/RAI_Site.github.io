On **September 9, RAI for Ukraine** kicked off the 2026–27 academic year with its annual Open House, welcoming a new cohort of research fellows and hearing from new and returning mentors about the projects they will lead this year. This fall, **32 research fellows** will work with **28 mentors** on **15 research projects**.

As the program enters its fifth year, its achievements since launching in 2022 reflect the growth of an international research community:

- **141 research fellows** from **19 Ukrainian universities**, including this year's cohort
- **81 mentors** representing **40 academic institutions** across **13 countries**
- **57 research projects** completed
- **24 peer-reviewed publications** to date, with several more in the pipeline
