**Administrative Director, Center for Responsible AI & Academic AI Initiatives**

{% include figure.html people-profile=true path="assets/img/people/anya.png" title="anya" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

Anya Kozorez is the Administrative Director for the NYU Center for Responsible AI and Academic AI Initiatives. She oversees the Center's program development, communications, and operations while supporting AI adoption and curriculum innovation across NYU Tandon. Before joining NYU, she spent more than 20 years in educational publishing, leading editorial strategy and product development for nationally recognized learning resources.
