Julia is Professor of Computer Science & Engineering at the NYU Tandon School of Engineering, Professor of Data Science at the Courant Institute School of Mathematics, Computing, and Data Science, Associate Dean of AI Initiatives at NYU Tandon, and Director of the Center for Responsible AI (R/AI). Julia’s goal is to make “Responsible AI” synonymous with “AI”. She works toward this goal through academic research, education and training, and technology policy, and by speaking about the benefits and harms of AI with practitioners, policymakers, and members of the public.

Julia’s research interests include data management and AI systems, AI ethics, and legal compliance. In addition to academic publications, she has written for The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, and Le Monde. Julia teaches courses on responsible data science and AI to students, practitioners, and the general public, and develops educational programs for industry and government. She is a co-author of Data, Responsibly, an award-winning comic book series for data science enthusiasts, and We Are AI, a comic book series for the general audience.

Julia is engaged in technology policy and regulation in the U.S. and internationally, having served on the New York City Automated Decision Systems Task Force, by mayoral appointment, among other roles.

Julia received her M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from Columbia University, and a B.S. in Computer Science and in Mathematics & Statistics from the University of Massachusetts at Amherst. She is the recipient of the Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers (PECASE), an NSF CAREER Award, and is a Senior Member of the ACM.
