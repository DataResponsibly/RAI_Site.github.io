NYU Tandon launched [AI Across the Curriculum](https://engineering.nyu.edu/events/2026/10/02/ai-across-curriculum-tandon-forum), a school-wide initiative led by [Julia Stoyanovich](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/julia-stoyanovich) to develop a human-centered approach to AI in engineering education. The forum brought together faculty and students to share progress, gather feedback, and identify priorities for the work ahead.
